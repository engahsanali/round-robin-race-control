Privacy

Round Robin Race Manager at ahsantechlabs.com/roundrobin · Last updated 22 November 2026

Round Robin Race Manager is a free tool made by Ahsan Ali (AhsanTechLabs) for running club race days. This page explains what it keeps about you and why.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, name and optional nickname. If you use a password, we keep only a salted one-way hash of it, never the password itself. If you sign in with Google, we keep your Google account ID so we can recognise you next time.
  • Sign-up and sign-in details: when and how you signed up (email or Google), the IP address you signed up from, when you last signed in, how many times you've signed in, and a short history of recent account activity (for example sign-ins and password resets) with IP addresses. Active sign-in sessions also record the browser's user agent.
  • Your race data: everything you enter, such as events, venues, dates, entrants' names and car numbers, brackets, results, podiums, points, fees and payment notes, and your settings.
  • Public displays: if you publish an event's display, anyone with that link can see it until you revoke the link.

Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address, whether Google has verified it, your name and your Google account ID. We use these only to sign you in and to show your name in the app. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

How it's used and shared

  • Your data is used only to run Race Manager for you. It is never sold, and it isn't used for advertising or profiling.
  • There are no ads or third-party analytics or trackers. We use cookies only to keep you signed in, to protect Google sign-in, and for the owner's admin session.
  • The data is stored on a private server rented from Hostinger. We don't share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.
  • If we start sending emails such as password resets, an email delivery provider will receive only what's needed to deliver that message.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and race data until you ask us to delete it. Deleting just a login from the admin dashboard does not delete race data. Backups and past admin audit records may keep data for a while after it is removed from the live app; tell us if your request should include them.

Your choices and deleting your data

To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and race data, email contact@ahsantechlabs.com. Please send it from the email address on your account so we know it's you. The owner handles these requests by hand. Signing in with Google can also be removed from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
